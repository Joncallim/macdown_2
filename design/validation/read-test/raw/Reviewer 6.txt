“MT.” The T is more immediately legible; the M feels compressed into it, so there is a little ambiguity at the join.

A motorsport or esports monogram. The forward lean and sharp cuts suggest speed and performance.
What kind of product or company might use it?
A gaming-hardware brand, a racing team, or a performance-sportswear company.

“MT,” more clearly than in S1. The blue helps separate the T from the dark M.
What did it remind you of?
A desktop application icon. The rounded tile makes it feel like software, although the lettering still has a sporty, racing-inspired character.
S3

A minimalist software-download website, probably for a standalone application or utility. The navigation establishes the software context much more clearly than the mark alone; the small logo still has an esports or motorsport flavour.
