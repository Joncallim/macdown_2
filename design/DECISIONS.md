The `ceil(0.75 n)` scaling is removed. 16 px gate stays at 6 of 8; automotive trigger stays at 3 or more participants | Owner decision: first exposure is the only clean measure of spontaneous reading, and a fixed panel keeps the threshold from drifting with sample size | 2026-09-25 · accepted (owner); formal panel not run: gate waived by the owner (D-026), not passed |

| D-027 | **Production lean: 10°**, superseding the 12° in D-011. Direct comparison of 8°/10°/12° in Icon Composer (`design/evidence/2026-09-26/icon-art/README.md`) found 10° keeps most of Slant's forward energy while visibly softening the diagonal "speed stripe" read that drove D-021's motorsport-association risk; 8° reads as flat and static, losing the reason Slant was chosen over Caret (D-010). Only the lean angle changes — wedge cut, diagonal:stem ratio (15:17) and all other D-011 construction parameters are untouched. **The derive-from-master correction (approved 2026-09-26, D-026) is accepted** for `slant-16.svg`, `slant-icon-16-inner.svg` and `slant-32.svg`: direct comparison in Icon Composer confirmed the derived candidates are visibly bolder and closer to the master's fused silhouette than the current hand-lightened drawings, corroborating `design/validation/round-2/DIAGNOSIS.md`'s pixel-match findings. **Finding, not a rule change:** rendered through Icon Composer's actual production pipeline (not the design lane's canvas-rasterisation diagnostic), the master geometry alone reads clearly as "MT" at true 16 px in Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted appearances — the macOS **app icon** does not need a separate microglyph; the small-size correction above still applies to `slant-16.svg`/`slant-32.svg`/`slant-icon-16-inner.svg` for contexts outside Icon Composer's renderer (favicon, in-app chrome), where no such rescue exists. Gate 2 (macOS icon set, D-017) is **not yet closed**: wiring the `.icon` file into the app and checking a live Dock/Finder icon hit a toolchain gap (`design/evidence/2026-09-26/icon-art/README.md`, "Toolchain finding") — `xcodebuild`/`actool` on this machine silently fails to compile an Icon-Composer-format `.icon` catalog member. **The lean-angle change is not yet cascaded.** Per D-021's own warning, changing the lean touches the wordmark slant, the lockup, the small drawings and the icon sources; only the mark comparison and the icon geometry above were executed. `design/brand/slant/production/` (geometry.json, the wordmark/lockup build scripts, D-024's Figma file) is still built at 12° and has not been regenerated at 10°; the checked-in `slant-*.svg` validation files are still 12° too. This decision authorises that cascade; it does not perform it. The geometry judgment (10° lean, derive-from-master correction) is final; the remaining work is (a) a build-tooling fix for Gate 2 and (b) regenerating the production wordmark, lockup, Figma outlines and validation SVGs at 10°, neither of which is a design question | Direct, real-render comparison superseded the original D-011 rationale ("12° keeps the energy without reading as sport"), which round-1 evidence had already partly contradicted. The bounded correction preserves the construction the owner chose while responding to the actual measured risk | 2026-09-26 · accepted (lean and small-size correction as design judgments); production cascade and Gate 2 still open |
