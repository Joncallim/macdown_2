Privacy

MostlyText is a local editor. Your documents stay on your Mac.

Your documents

Opening, editing, saving, preview, math, diagrams and export all run on your Mac and work without an internet connection. MostlyText doesn't send document content to any service.

A text filter you install yourself runs as a local command on your Mac, and it only receives the text you choose to pass to it.

Update checks

MostlyText checks mostlytext.app for new versions. That request includes your app version and macOS version, and no document content. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings.

Analytics

MostlyText doesn't include analytics, tracking or advertising code.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. The host keeps standard web server logs.
